Functional requirements - what the system should do
Non-functional requirements - what are the constraints on the system/development
- Data requirements
	- Where is data coming from, how to store it, how long will it be stored
- Environmental
	- Physical characteristics, social characteristics, organisational, technical
- User characteristics
	- ICT related abilities, age, nationality, education, disabliity
- Usability goals and user experience requirements
	- Effectiveness of usability, entertainment factor of UX
