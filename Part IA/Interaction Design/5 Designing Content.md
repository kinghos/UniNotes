#### Information architecture
The practice of deciding how to arrange the parts of something to be understandable. A good IA helps people to understand their surroundings and find what they're looking for - in the real world as well as online.
Aims to
- Organise content into taxonomies and hierarchies
- Define relationships between content and functionality
- Tag content with metadata

1. Inventory content
2. Establish a taxonomy (structure)
3. Chunking
4. Diagram
5. Analyse - test with real users and iterate
A taxonomy is a system for organising things that share similar characteristics into groups, and naming groups
Types:
- Flat (enumerations)
- Hierarchy (is-a relationship)
- Faceted (has-a relationship)
- Network (connected content e.g. tags)
Navigation can go up/down or left/right in hierarchy

Model data with the user in mind
