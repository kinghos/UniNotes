The joint probability mass function of two discrete random variables $X$ and $Y$ is the function $p:\mathbb{R}^2\to[0,1]$ defined by
$$p_{X,Y}=P[X=a,Y=b]$$
The joint distribution function follows similarly, with $P[X\leq a,Y\leq b]$
The marginal distribution of $X$ is
$$F_{X}(a)=P[X\leq a]=\lim_{ b \to \infty }F_{X,Y}(a,b) $$
Variables $X$ and $Y$ have a joint continuous distribution as follows
$$P[a_{1}\leq X\leq b_{1},a_{2}\leq Y\leq b_{2}]=\int^{b_{1}}_{a_{1}}\int^{b_{2}}_{a_{2}}f(x,y)\,dx\,dy$$
where $f(x,y)\leq{0}$
