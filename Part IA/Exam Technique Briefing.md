- Avoid assuming that a question will be the same as a PPQ if there is a resemblance
- Command words:
	- State - give a fact, no explanation
	- Explain - give reasoning
	- Prove - rigorous mathematical or logical steps
	- Trace - step-by-step execution state
- Check the scope - what are the bounds/limitations
- State assumptions for safety

##### What the examiner means
- Try and figure out what specific topic the examiner is talking about
- Figure out requirements from the mark count
- Point-Proof-Comment format to match solution notes
- Make marks visible e.g. draw box around final solution
- Make your answer "look correct" - if you held it at arms length, would it seem visibly correct

##### Precision, structure, avoiding waffle
- Plan before you write - particularly for algorithmic/design questions
- Use diagrams and ALWAYS label axes and components
- Be really specific, state lots of detail even for things that may seem obvious

##### Time management
- Spend 10 minutes choosing questions
- Aim for 14.5 for a first - the more marks you get in the upper areas has a stronger effect on your ranking
- **Only cross out if you have fully written a better version**
