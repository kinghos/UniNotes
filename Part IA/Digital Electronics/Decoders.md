A decoder is similar to a [[Multiplexers and Demultiplexers#Demultiplexers|demultiplexer]], where $g$ is permanently connected to logic 1. This yields a 1-of-2 decoder (1:2 decoder). This means only one output is logic 1 at a time.
![[DecoderExample.png]]
Here, $x=1, y=z=0$ would enable system 1 (an input of $001$, read from z->x)

Decoders can be used output combinational logic.
