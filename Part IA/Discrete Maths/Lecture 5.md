Every natural number i not a multiple of a prime number p has a reciprocal modulo p, namely $i^{p-2}$ as $i\cdot(i^{p-2})\equiv_{1}(\mod p)$

#### Negation
Statements of the form not P or P is not the case or P is absurd or P leads to contradiction or $\lnot P$
