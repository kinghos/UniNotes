### Numbers
Monoid laws - $0+n=n=n+0, (l+m)+n=l+(m+n)$
Commutativity law - $m+n=n+m$

A monoid is an algebraic structure with 
- a neutral element, say $e$
- a binary operation, say $\bullet$
satisfying
- neutral element laws: $e\bullet x = x = x \bullet e$
- $(x\bullet y)\bullet z = x\bullet(y\bullet z)$
A monoid is commutative if $x\bullet y = y \bullet x$
